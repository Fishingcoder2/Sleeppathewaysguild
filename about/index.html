About the Guild

Free learning support for the craft of sleep technology.

Sleep Pathways Guild is an independent, peer-led educational project founded by Tracy Frazier, RHIT, RPSGT, CCS-P. It was created to keep practical sleep-technology learning within reach for CPSGT and RPSGT learners, students, working technologists, and professionals strengthening their skills.

What the Guild provides

The public resources include original practice questions, mock-style exams, guided study paths, sleep-scoring review, EKG learning, technical calculations, flashcards, skill labs, progress tools, articles, and printable study resources.

How the Guild approaches education

The Guild emphasizes standards-aware study, technical reasoning, signal quality, patient-centered monitoring, responsible escalation, accurate scoring habits, and clear explanations. Official organizations and current manuals remain the source for credential requirements, scoring rules, technical guidance, and examination policies.

Independent status

Sleep Pathways Guild is not a school, credentialing authority, medical provider, or substitute for formal education, clinical supervision, employer policy, official manuals, or professional judgment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by BRPT, AASM, AAST, textbook publishers, Amazon, or other organizations unless specifically stated.
