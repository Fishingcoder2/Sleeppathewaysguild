Know the price before you apply

Tutoring session pricing

$65minimum per booked session

The standard session covers the first 60 minutes, including tutoring and related notes, organization, and the session summary. If you end the meeting after 30 minutes, the $65 minimum still applies. A tutor may use 45 minutes for the meeting and the remaining time for notes and the summary.

Additional time: $17.50 per additional 15-minute billing increment. The first additional increment applies when the session runs longer than 68 minutes; the next applies after 83 minutes, then after 98 minutes, and so on.

Appointment fee: a $25 no-show or cancellation fee applies to a paid appointment missed or canceled with less than 24 hours’ notice, once per appointment. Complimentary beta students are not charged tuition or these appointment fees under their approved beta terms.

Optional materials and separate test-review services have their own prices. Any additional service will be discussed before it is billed.

Before you begin

Have these details ready.

  • Your CPSGT or RPSGT exam date, or your expected test window.
  • Whether you have taken the exam before and have a previous score report.
  • Your education level, sleep-technology training, and the program or pathway provider you are studying with.
  • The concepts you struggle with, the help you want, and your available days and times with your time zone.
  • Any learning or English-language support you want us to consider. These questions are optional.

Have you used the free webapps?

The intake asks what you have completed and whether you can share your study report.

  • Study trail completed: share the report with Coach Bob feedback, if available.
  • Practice only: share the practice report without Coach Bob after completing at least 500 questions. This is the Guild’s benchmark for a broader practice history.

You can still apply if you do not yet have an eligible report. Tell us where you are in your study process. Report sharing is arranged privately with the administrator.
