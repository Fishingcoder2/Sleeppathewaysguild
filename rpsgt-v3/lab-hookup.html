Recommended EEG

F4-M1 · C4-M1 · O2-M1

Adult routine sleep staging uses frontal, central, and occipital coverage. Backup electrodes F3, C3, O1, and M2 support contralateral substitution when needed.

Recommended EOG

E1-M2 · E2-M2

E1 is positioned below and lateral to the left outer canthus; E2 is positioned above and lateral to the right outer canthus.

Chin EMG

Three-electrode submental setup

One superior midline electrode and two inferior electrodes on opposite sides provide a working derivation plus backup capability.

Impedance

Know the channel-specific target

EEG, EOG, and chin EMG measured electrode impedance: maximum 5 kΩ. Limb EMG: 10 kΩ or less is acceptable, while 5 kΩ or less is preferred.

Respiratory hookup

Match the sensor to the scoring purpose

For adult diagnostic PSG, oronasal thermal airflow is the recommended apnea airflow signal; nasal pressure is the recommended hypopnea airflow signal.
