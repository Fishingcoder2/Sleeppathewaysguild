Why source roles matter
Coach Bob Exam Reasoning Compass

Four checks before you commit to the answer

Use on scenario questions

The compass is a repeatable way to slow down just enough to find the technically defensible answer without turning every question into a guessing contest.

1
Patient First

Check the person, order, immediate safety concern, and technologist scope before the screen takes over.

2
Signal Family

Identify the channel or physiologic system before naming the event, artifact, or intervention.

3
Cross-Check

Correlate related channels, history, device data, or observations rather than trusting one noisy clue.

4
Proof Clue

Name the specific feature, rule, relationship, or calculation that makes the best answer defensible.

How answers are governed

Use the strongest applicable authority first

When content is rule-sensitive or version-sensitive, V3 should identify the controlling official or professional source before using textbooks or supplemental resources for reinforcement.

1BRPT official exam informationBlueprint, Candidate Handbook, eligibility, policies, and official exam information
2Current AASM scoring & applicable guidanceScoring rules and applicable diagnostic or treatment guidance
3Applicable AAST guidanceTechnical and professional guidance for sleep technologist practice
4BRPT-listed textbooks & core referencesDeeper learning and reinforcement after applicable professional guidance
5Supplemental educational resourcesAdditional context only when provenance and support are appropriate
No invented provenance: A resource is not mapped to a question merely because it is broadly related to AASM, AAST, sleep medicine, or the same general topic.
