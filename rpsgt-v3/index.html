How answers are governed

Official source first, textbook second

When a topic is rule-sensitive or version-sensitive, the learning pathway should follow the controlling exam or professional authority before reinforcement from a textbook or supplemental resource.

1BRPT blueprint & candidate handbookExam domains, tasks, format, and policies
2Current AASM scoring & official guidanceScoring rules and applicable diagnostic or treatment guidance
3BRPT-listed primary referencesFundamentals and other listed texts for deeper understanding
4AAST technical guidance & supplemental sourcesTechnologist workflow, technical reinforcement, and clinical enrichment where applicable
Independent preparation tool: Sleep Pathways Guild is not the BRPT and does not represent BRPT endorsement. Learners should always verify current eligibility, examination policies, and official source versions with the credentialing organization.
