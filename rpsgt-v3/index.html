Coach Bob Exam Reasoning Compass

Four checks before you commit to the answer

1
Patient First

Check the person, order, immediate safety concern, and technologist scope before the screen takes over.

2
Signal Family

Identify the channel or physiologic system before naming the event, artifact, or intervention.

3
Cross-Check

Correlate related channels, history, device data, or observations rather than trusting one noisy clue.

4
Proof Clue

Name the specific feature, rule, relationship, or calculation that makes the best answer defensible.
