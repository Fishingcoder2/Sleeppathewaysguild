Do not just name the event. Prove why you scored it.

Use the complete channel relationship and timing context. A scoring label should follow the evidence, not replace the evidence.

Evidence boundary: The staging epochs, respiratory traces, scoring-context traces, and consecutive-epoch runs are original Sleep Pathways Guild schematic teaching signals, not patient recordings or copied source figures. Phase 3 · Consecutive-epoch skill

Stage short runs without losing neighboring context

4 runs · 12 epoch decisions

Each run keeps three full 30-second schematic epochs together. Stage one epoch at a time while the neighboring traces remain available for context. First answers count, and 80% or higher records a Phase 3 practice pass. The sequence order is pedagogic and does not imply that every patient must progress through stages in a fixed pattern.

Phase 3 validation boundary: This consecutive-epoch practice is saved separately while the interaction is being validated. It does not yet add a new Scoring Lab completion requirement.
Interactive event-evidence skill

Classify the respiratory event, then prove the score on the tracing

5 cases · 10 evidence targets

For each original 2-minute 30-second teaching trace, commit the event classification first. Then click two waveform findings that support the intended score. A wrong evidence click stays on the same target and keeps the hint visible. “Show me” highlights the target but does not auto-complete it.

Interactive scoring-context skill

Decide what counts — and what does not

8 cases · 16 supporting clues

Work through dedicated arousal, limb-movement, artifact-versus-physiology, and transition/boundary cases. Commit the scoring decision first, then identify both findings that support that decision. Longer movement and boundary cases preserve enough surrounding time to judge association rather than reading an isolated burst.
