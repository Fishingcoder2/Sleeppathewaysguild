Phase 3 · Consecutive-epoch skill

Stage short runs without losing neighboring context

4 runs · 12 epoch decisions

Each run keeps three full 30-second schematic epochs together. On a desktop computer, the three neighboring epoch cards appear side by side; smaller screens stack them for readability. Swipe or drag left/right inside any tracing to move through the epoch; all three neighboring traces stay synchronized so you are comparing the same time position. Stage one epoch at a time while the neighboring traces remain available for context. Apply Version 3 criteria to the current epoch; a neighboring stage is context, not a substitute for the evidence in the epoch being scored. First answers count, and 80% or higher records a Phase 3 practice pass. The sequence order is pedagogic and does not imply that every patient must progress through stages in a fixed pattern.

Phase 3 validation boundary: This consecutive-epoch practice is saved separately while the interaction is being validated. It does not add a new Scoring Lab completion requirement.
Phase 3 · PSG-style annotation across epoch boundaries

Drag a bar from physiologic onset through recovery

4 cases · 8 annotations

Each case shows one continuous 90-second respiratory strip divided visually into three 30-second epochs. Choose the nasal-pressure or thermistor row and drag across every distinct candidate airflow reduction you identify. This skill tests onset, continuity, recovery, and placement—not whether each generic reduction independently satisfies all Version 3 apnea or hypopnea criteria. If there are two reductions, place two bars; if there are three, place three. A vertical epoch divider organizes the display; it does not turn one continuous physiologic reduction into two. Seven of the eight first-pass annotations are required for an 80% Phase 3 practice pass.

Phase 3 validation boundary: Boundary annotation is saved separately while the interaction is being validated. Actual respiratory-event classification belongs to the Version 3 rule-based respiratory exercises and requires the relevant airflow, duration, effort, desaturation/arousal, and subtype evidence. This placement skill does not add a new Scoring Lab completion requirement.
Phase 3 · Scored mini-study

Score a sequence, then stop when the full montage changes the question

12 epochs · 3 context stops · 15 decisions

Work through 12 consecutive 30-second schematic PSG epochs while the previous and next epochs remain visible. After selected epochs, three full-montage context stops interrupt the stage sequence so you must apply Version 3 reasoning to an N2 arousal, a longer periodic-limb-movement series, and an adult obstructive apnea that remains continuous across a 30-second epoch boundary. First answers count; corrections are required before progression; 12 of 15 first-pass decisions is exactly 80%.

Phase 3 completion boundary: The scored mini-study has its own durable pass and attempt history, but it remains separate from the locked Scoring Lab completion gate. Existing completion behavior is unchanged.
Interactive event-evidence skill

Classify the respiratory event, then prove the score on the tracing

5 cases · 10 evidence targets

For each original 2-minute 30-second teaching trace, apply the adult AASM Version 3 respiratory framework and commit the event classification first. Then click two waveform findings that support the intended score. The cases require the appropriate relationship among the recommended airflow signal, duration, respiratory effort, oxygen or arousal consequence, and event subtype. A wrong evidence click stays on the same target and keeps the hint visible. “Show me” highlights the target but does not auto-complete it.

Interactive scoring-context skill

Decide what counts — and what does not

8 cases · 16 supporting clues

Work through dedicated Version 3-based arousal, limb-movement, artifact-versus-physiology, and transition/boundary cases. Commit the scoring decision first, then identify both findings that support that decision.
