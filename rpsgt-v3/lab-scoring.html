PSG Workstation · Sleep staging and event scoring

Review the tracing like a sleep technologist

Move through seven workstation stations: stage recognition, consecutive-epoch transitions, arousal recognition, respiratory-event classification, limb-movement context, artifact versus physiology, and population/protocol boundaries. Each station asks you to inspect the signal, commit to a score or protocol decision, and prove it with supporting evidence before the teaching answer appears.

The workstation is waveform-first. Use the event, stage, and protocol controls only after you have checked the complete signal pathway and surrounding context.

Training-workstation boundary: This is an original Sleep Pathways Guild educational interface inspired by common PSG review-workstation conventions. It is not Compumedics, Neurovirtual, or another clinical recording system and is not intended for patient acquisition or diagnosis. Visual practicums use app-authored teaching signals; rule-sensitive decisions should be verified against current official AASM guidance.
