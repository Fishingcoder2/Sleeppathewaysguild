Source authority & learner protection

What this Reference Center shows—and what it intentionally hides

BRPT · Exam frameworkExam blueprint, handbook, eligibility, candidate, and credential information. This orients exam preparation; it is not a clinical scoring hierarchy.
1 · AASM scoring / technicalGoverning scoring rules and scoring-linked technical specifications. Use first when the issue is how an event, montage, technical specification, or report element is scored or defined.
2 · AASM accreditation / protocolFacility protocol, accreditation, infection-control, and written-procedure requirements. These do not replace clinical or scoring rules.
3 · AASM clinical / protocol / classificationCurrent diagnostic, treatment, testing-protocol, and classification guidance for the clinical question at hand.
4 · AAST technical / competency / scopeCurrent technologist workflow, competency, professional-role, and scope guidance. Use beneath controlling AASM rules and above textbooks for procedural application.
5 · Core referencesBRPT-listed textbooks and other core reinforcement references used after the controlling authority is identified.
6 · SupplementalAdditional specialty, visual, historical, or educational context that must not outrank current authority.
Local and device-specific overlayPhysician orders, facility SOPs and emergency policies, state law, and manufacturer instructions remain required when execution is patient-specific, local, or device-specific. The Reference Center does not invent universal local thresholds or device procedures.
Oxygen boundarySupplemental low-flow oxygen is treated as indication- and order-specific. The current audit does not identify a standalone AAST low-flow oxygen technical guideline, so the app must not invent a universal oxygen titration protocol.
PAP pathway boundaryOSA PAP/CPAP-BPAP, adult CSA/ASV, and hypoventilation/NIV are kept as separate authority pathways. Older or generic PAP material must not silently control a different treatment pathway.
Pediatric boundaryAAST Standard PSG is not presented as pediatric PSG authority because its stated scope excludes pediatric PSG. Current AASM infant/pediatric rules, applicable pediatric protocols, age-specific competency, facility policy, and supporting pediatric references are layered separately.
Citation integrityExplicit APA citations are displayed as stored. When a vetted source record contains another verified citation style, it is labeled “Recorded source citation” rather than silently rewritten.
Copyright boundaryThe center shows bibliographic information, original study directions, and section or chapter titles. It does not reproduce protected textbook prose, scoring rules, figures, tables, or proprietary question banks.
Private library boundaryInternal Drive URLs, holdings records, audit notes, QA states, and library-management data remain hidden from learners.
Current-source warningCertification requirements, scoring rules, protocols, and professional guidance can change. Version-sensitive material should be verified against the current controlling source.
Independent educational useSleep Pathways Guild is independent. References to BRPT, AASM, AAST, publishers, or professional literature do not imply endorsement.
Educational achievementsGuild badges, medals, practice scores, readiness results, and mock results are educational measures only—not BRPT credentials, official scores, or passing predictions.

Scores & readinessPractice, Readiness, Mock, Guided Study, reports, badges, and progress indicators are educational measures only and are not official BRPT scores, credential decisions, or passing predictions.
Question originSleep Pathways Guild practice questions are original educational items, not actual BRPT examination questions or copied proprietary question-bank material.
CopyrightStudy recommendations, outlines, citations, and objectives point learners toward references without reproducing substantial protected passages, proprietary scoring rules, figures, tables, or question banks.
Affiliate disclosureSome optional commercial destinations elsewhere in the Guild may use affiliate links. A standard disclosure is shown to learners, while internal tracking values are not displayed as learner-facing content. External linksExternal sites control their own content, availability, privacy practices, and current versions. Learners should verify current requirements and guidance at the authoritative destination.
AI assistance & human reviewAI tools may assist development, organization, coding, or source discovery. Production and rule-sensitive content still require source validation and human review.
