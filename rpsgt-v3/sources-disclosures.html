Sources & Disclosures

RPSGT V3 is an independent educational and exam-preparation system. This page explains source authority, question origin, score limitations, local learner data, external links, commercial relationships, and content review boundaries.

Coach Bob

Know what controls the answer.

When a rule or protocol matters, use the current controlling professional source. Study tools can explain and reinforce the rule, but they should never quietly replace it.

Independence

Sleep Pathways Guild is not the Board of Registered Polysomnographic Technologists (BRPT) and is not affiliated with or endorsed by BRPT. References to BRPT credentials, domains, tasks, handbooks, blueprints, or other BRPT resources are for educational orientation and exam-preparation alignment.

AASM & AAST

Use of American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) or American Association of Sleep Technologists (AAST) names, publications, standards, guidelines, or educational resources does not imply endorsement of Sleep Pathways Guild. Current applicable professional guidance should control when rule-sensitive content is involved.

Educational use

This learning center is for education and exam preparation. It is not a substitute for institutional policy, provider orders, current professional standards, clinical judgment, supervision, licensure requirements, or patient-specific medical decision-making.

Scores & readiness

Practice, Readiness, Mock, Guided Study, reports, badges, and progress indicators are educational measures only. They are not official BRPT score reports, credential decisions, passing predictions, employer records, or clinical records.

Current-source warning

Certification requirements, exam policies, professional standards, clinical guidelines, and scoring rules can change. Learners should verify current BRPT requirements and current AASM, AAST, and other applicable professional guidance before relying on version-sensitive information.

Question origin

Sleep Pathways Guild practice questions are original educational items created for study. They are not actual BRPT examination questions and are not copied from proprietary BRPT exams, protected textbook question banks, or proprietary scoring-manual question sets.

Copyright

Study recommendations, outlines, citations, learning objectives, and source mappings are intended to point learners toward appropriate references without reproducing proprietary books, the AASM Scoring Manual, copyrighted question banks, or substantial protected passages.

Affiliate disclosure

Some optional book or product destinations may use affiliate links. When an affiliate relationship applies, Sleep Pathways Guild should disclose it before or around the commercial link. Official BRPT, AASM, AAST, government, publisher, and free public resources should remain visually distinguishable from commercial destinations.

Privacy & local learner data

RPSGT V3 learner progress is designed to be stored locally in the learner's browser unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise. Printable and exported study reports exclude question text, answer text, rationales, private notes, learner searches, private links, and raw browser state. Learner name is opt-in.

External links

External destinations may be official professional resources, free public resources, publisher resources, testing-partner resources, or commercial destinations. V3 should label these roles clearly. External websites control their own content, availability, privacy practices, and current versions.

AI assistance & human review

AI tools may assist with drafting, organization, coding, source discovery, or educational content development. AI assistance does not replace clinical, technical, editorial, or source verification. Rule-sensitive and production content should remain subject to human review and current-source validation.

Content errors & support

If you find questionable content, a broken resource link, a suspected scoring issue, or a technical error, please report it so the item can be reviewed instead of silently corrected without provenance.
