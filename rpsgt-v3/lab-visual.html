Development prototype · Visual Skills Engine

Work with the tracing, not a screenshot of a tracing

The first Mini PSG Viewer renders an original 30-second schematic sleep-study epoch from signal definitions, then reuses that same epoch for stage recognition and feature-location questions. This is the foundation for future full-night scoring, respiratory-event marking, artifact recognition, measurements, and disagreement review.

Read across channels before you decide.

Use the complete displayed context. A visual scoring tool should teach you to correlate signals and features—not train you to guess from one isolated squiggle.

Prototype and clinical boundary: The displayed signals are deterministic, original Sleep Pathways Guild schematic teaching signals. They are not a patient recording, copied scoring-manual figure, textbook figure, or validated substitute for current official scoring criteria. This prototype tests the viewer and interaction architecture; it is not yet a completed scoring competency lab.
