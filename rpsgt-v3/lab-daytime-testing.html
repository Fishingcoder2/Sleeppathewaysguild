Protocol and copyright boundary: Guided activities are original Sleep Pathways Guild educational content grounded in current AASM protocol papers. They do not reproduce the AASM protocol boxes, scoring manual text, diagnostic criteria, proprietary tables, report templates, or copied figures. Clinical testing must follow current AASM guidance, the active order, facility policy, and supervised competency. The 2024 pediatric AASM paper states that evidence is insufficient to recommend a pediatric MWT protocol; this lab therefore teaches adult MWT purpose and pediatric limitations rather than pediatric normative interpretation. Lab results remain isolated from ordinary Practice, Readiness, Mock, remediation, and Guided Trail history.
