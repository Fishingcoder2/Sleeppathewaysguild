How to choose

Buy for the learning gap, not for the size of the shelf.

RPSGT preparation is stronger when a book has a clear job. A broad reference can anchor your study plan, while a technical, pediatric, respiratory, visual, or ECG title can deepen a specific weak area.

Broad RPSGT review

Start with a broad sleep-technology reference when you need one book that connects physiology, patient care, PSG, therapy, and professional practice.

Instrumentation & troubleshooting

Choose a technical polysomnography title when sensors, filters, acquisition, artifact, signal quality, monitoring, or procedures are the weak area.

Pediatric sleep

Use pediatric references for development, family-centered assessment, pediatric PSG context, and age-specific clinical reasoning.

Clinical cases

Case-based books are useful for connecting findings to the larger clinical story and practicing best-answer reasoning.

Respiratory & PAP depth

A respiratory sleep reference can add gas exchange, hypoventilation, PAP, and cardiopulmonary context.

Visual & ECG review

Atlases and ECG references support pattern recognition that complements, rather than replaces, current scoring standards and formal training.

Books support study. Current standards still control.
