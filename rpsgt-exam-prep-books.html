Choose by learning need

You do not need every book.

Broad RPSGT foundation

Start with Fundamentals for Sleep Professionals or Fundamentals of Sleep Technology when you want one wide reference covering many sleep-technology domains.

Instrumentation & troubleshooting

Choose Polysomnography for the Sleep Technologist when sensors, filters, acquisition, signal quality, artifacts, monitoring, or technical procedures are weak areas.

Pediatric sleep

Use the pediatric titles when you need stronger developmental, family-centered, PSG, or pediatric clinical reasoning.

Clinical cases

Sleep Medicine Pearls and Pediatric Sleep Pearls are useful when you learn best by connecting findings to case context.

Respiratory depth

The ERS handbook can add respiratory testing, gas exchange, hypoventilation, PAP, and cardiopulmonary context.

Visual & ECG review

The sleep atlas supports visual recognition, while the EKG reference helps reinforce cardiac rhythm fundamentals relevant to the sleep lab.

Study hierarchy

Books support study. Current standards still control.

Textbooks explain concepts in depth, but editions age. When a detail is version-sensitive, verify it against the current authoritative source.

  • AASM Scoring Manual: current scoring rules, terminology, technical specifications, and event definitions.
  • BRPT: current RPSGT candidate information, eligibility, examination policies, blueprint, and reference information.
  • AAST and other current professional guidance: technical-practice guidance where applicable.
  • Employer policy and clinical leadership: facility-specific procedures and escalation requirements.

Frequently asked questions

RPSGT book and study questions

Do I need to buy every RPSGT exam prep book?

No. Start with current official standards and the areas where your knowledge is weakest, then choose a book only when it adds useful depth.

Which book is strongest for instrumentation?

Polysomnography for the Sleep Technologist is especially useful for sensors, filters, signal acquisition, artifact, monitoring, troubleshooting, safety, and technical procedures.

Which books help with pediatric sleep?

A Clinical Guide to Pediatric Sleep, Pediatric Sleep Pearls, and Principles and Practice of Pediatric Sleep Medicine provide different levels of pediatric clinical and PSG context.

Do these books replace the AASM Scoring Manual?

No. Use textbooks for understanding and context. Use the current AASM Scoring Manual and other current official sources for version-sensitive rules and requirements.
