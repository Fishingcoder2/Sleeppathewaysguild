Which RPSGT study book should you start with?

Broad foundation

Start with Fundamentals of Sleep Technology when you want one wide reference spanning most RPSGT learning areas.

Instrumentation & troubleshooting

Use Polysomnography for the Sleep Technologist when electronics, filters, sensors, signals, artifacts, or technical procedures are weak areas.

Pediatric sleep

Use Pediatric Sleep Pearls or A Clinical Guide to Pediatric Sleep for pediatric development, PSG context, clinical presentation, and family-centered reasoning.

Books support study. Current standards still control.

Textbooks explain concepts in depth, but they age. Use the current AASM Scoring Manual for current scoring rules, BRPT for current credential and exam information, current AAST and other professional guidance where applicable, and employer policy for facility-specific procedures.
