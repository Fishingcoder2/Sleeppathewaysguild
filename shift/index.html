The Shift Report

Original, human-reviewed reporting for sleep technologists. The new Shift Report will translate clinical, technical, workforce, credentialing, and industry developments into practical implications for the people preparing, acquiring, monitoring, troubleshooting, scoring, and managing sleep studies.

Migration status: Automated scraping and Tumblr publishing have been retired. This page is a staging hub and does not carry advertising. It will be opened to search indexing after original issues, verified continuing-education resources, and reviewed job listings are published.

What the new Shift will maintain

External sources will be used as research leads. Published material will add original clinical context, limitations, workflow implications, source attribution, and human editorial review.

Editorial standards

  • No scraped or lightly rewritten articles.
  • No automatic public posting.
  • Primary and authoritative sources whenever available.
  • Original explanation of what each development means on shift.
  • Clear separation of guidance, evidence, manufacturer information, reporting, and editorial interpretation.
  • Human review before publication, with additional clinical review for high-risk subjects.
  • Jobs and CEC opportunities checked for accuracy, dates, conditions, and expiration.
  • Corrections, authorship, citations, disclosures, and educational limitations displayed clearly.
