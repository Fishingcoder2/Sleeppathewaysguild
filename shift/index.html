Editorial filter

Updates, not filler

Routine revision dates and unchanged policies are not treated as news. The emphasis is on new, changed, newly opened, newly closed, or time-sensitive information.

Editorial standards

  • Primary and authoritative sources whenever available.
  • No scraped or lightly rewritten articles.
  • Human review before publication.
  • Clear separation of factual notices and editorial interpretation.
  • Jobs, CEC opportunities, grants, deadlines, recalls, and links checked for current status.
  • Clinical and safety material kept within appropriate educational scope.
