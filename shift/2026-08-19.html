Featured Opportunity: AASM Foundation Travel Grant for Sleep Technologists

The AASM Foundation SLEEP Travel Grant for Sleep Technologists and Advanced Practice Providers is designed to support first-time attendance at the SLEEP meeting for non-physician members of the sleep team. The current page lists a $2,000 award and a December 15, 2026 application deadline.

Important: The current AASM Foundation page lists the December 2026 deadline while still referring to SLEEP 2026. Because that wording is internally inconsistent, The Shift Report is not labeling it a SLEEP 2027 grant until AASM Foundation clarifies or updates the page.

Speaker Shout-Out: Byron Jamerson

Byron Jamerson, RPSGT, RST, CCSH, FAAST is among this year's speakers. Byron has spent decades in sleep medicine and sleep technology education, and it is good to see experienced technologists and educators continuing to share that knowledge with the profession.

I have attended the Alabama conference in the past and enjoyed it. Regional meetings like this can offer something the very large national meetings sometimes cannot: a chance to sit down, talk with other sleep professionals, ask questions, and reconnect with people in the field.

Device Watch: Inspire Recall

This is an information-only safety notice. FDA lists 2026 Class II recalls involving Inspire Medical Systems Model 4340 respiratory sensing leads and Model 4063 stimulation leads. The issue involves an incorrect labeled use-by date extending beyond the validated shelf life. The corrective action is directed at affected clinical inventory.

This notice should not be confused with older Inspire recalls involving different devices or circumstances. The Shift Report is not adding independent clinical recommendations.

FDA Inspire recall event information

What We Are Watching Next

We are watching for the final 2027 Medicare Physician Fee Schedule, final implementation information for the new HST coding structure, clarification of the AASM Foundation travel grant page, Southern Sleep Society 2027 dates, new sleep-specific free CEC opportunities, substantive AASM/AAST/BRPT changes, current sleep-technologist job opportunities, scholarships and grants, and FDA safety notices involving equipment commonly encountered in sleep medicine.

From Tracy: Sleep medicine changes quickly, but not every new webpage, revision date, or announcement represents a meaningful change for the technologist working tonight. The goal of The Shift Report is to separate the things worth knowing from the noise, point you toward the original source whenever possible, and highlight opportunities that can actually help working sleep professionals.
