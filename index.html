The Guild mission

Learn the standards. Practice the craft. Help the next learner.

Sleep technology is careful work. The Guild is built around clean signals, patient-centered monitoring, technical reasoning, responsible escalation, accurate scoring habits, and a professional community that keeps the door open for learners.

Standards before shortcuts

Use current official references, strong source habits, and responsible study practices rather than memorized tricks alone.

Craft over noise

Signal quality, troubleshooting, scoring judgment, communication, and patient safety deserve serious attention.

Mentors, not gatekeepers

Knowledge grows when experienced technologists explain the work clearly and make space for serious learners.

Community with purpose

The Guild supports self-study, formal education, workplace learning, professional growth, and lifelong learning.

The people and purpose behind the Guild

Founded from experience, shaped by mentorship, and dedicated to sleep education.

Founded by Tracy Frazier, RHIT, RPSGT, CCS-P

Sleep Pathways Guild was founded and built by Tracy Frazier from her experience as a sleep technologist, health information professional, credentialed coding specialist, learner, and educator.

She created the Guild so serious learners could find practical explanations, free public tools, professional encouragement, and a place to begin without cost being the first obstacle.

Inspired by mentor Robert Dopson, RPSGT

Tracy’s mentor, Robert Dopson, RPSGT, never turned away an intern or serious learner. He taught in a Socratic way—helping people reason through a problem rather than simply handing them an answer.

His approach was to ask who, what, when, where, why, and to what extent before answering the question. That habit of careful inquiry remains part of the Guild’s educational foundation.

Honoring William C. Dement, MD, PhD

The Guild honors the memory of Dr. William C. Dement, a founder of sleep medicine, legendary teacher, and public advocate for sleep health and safety.

“Drowsiness is red alert.”William C. Dement, MD, PhD

His commitment to teaching the public that sleep matters inspires the Guild’s work to keep understandable sleep education available beyond professional walls.
