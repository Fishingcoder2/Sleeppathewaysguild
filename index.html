Sleep Pathways Guild

Support for your next step in sleep technology.

Sleep Pathways Guild is an independent learning-support project for students, sleep technologists, and professionals pursuing knowledge, workplace goals, continuing education, or credentials such as CPSGT, RPSGT, CCSH, and other sleep-related certifications.

Your pathway

Learn, practice, progress, and recognize achievement.

The Guild helps you organize your next step whether you are self-studying, completing online modules, enrolled in formal education, participating in an ASTEP or STAR program, or learning through workplace and clinical training. It is not a school, employer, or credentialing authority.
