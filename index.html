Sleep Pathways Guild

A guild for the craft of sleep technology.

Sleep Pathways Guild is an independent, peer-led educational project for sleep technologists, RPSGT learners, CPSGT learners, and CCSH-minded professionals who want structured study support, practical review, and standards-aware learning.

The Guild

A professional community built around the work.

Sleep technology is a craft. The Guild is being built around clean signals, careful monitoring, technical reasoning, correct escalation, and teaching that helps learners grow with confidence and humility.

Standards before shortcuts

We point learners toward official references, strong source habits, and responsible study practices.

Craft over noise

Monitoring skill, troubleshooting, scoring judgment, and patient safety deserve serious attention.

Mentors, not gatekeepers

Knowledge grows when experienced technologists keep the door open for serious learners.

Community with purpose

The project supports guided study, shared expertise, professional growth, and lifelong learning.
