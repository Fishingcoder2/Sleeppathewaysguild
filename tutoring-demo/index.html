Required references (study expectation)

  • Fundamentals of Sleep Technology, appropriate edition: course textbook; learner purchases/uses authorized copy.
  • Current AASM Manual for the Scoring of Sleep and Associated Events; official licensed edition needed.
  • Relevant AASM clinical practice guidelines for each domain/task.
  • Current BRPT candidate handbook and exam blueprint.

Highly recommended

  • AAST technical guidelines, mapped to the task.
  • Marie Sosa's sleep-technology books: confirm exact titles, editions and chapter mappings before assigning.
  • Other BRPT-recommended reading and references as applicable.
  • Guild original workbooks, Coach Bob teaching prompts and lab simulations.

Exact chapter/page citations are not guessed; assign them after checking the learner's edition and the Guild reference workbook.
