Request free mentorship

Email the administrator first.

Send your request to admin@sleeppathwaysguild.com. Include a short essay in your own words so the administrator can understand your goals and consider the limited seats.

  • What are you working toward in sleep technology?
  • Why would mentorship help you right now?
  • What guidance do you hope to receive?

You may also include your general availability and time zone. After reviewing your email, the administrator will explain any next steps and provide a request form when appropriate. Form answers are kept in the administrator’s restricted records.

Unpaid volunteer mentors

Help another learner take the next step.

We welcome volunteer applications from professionals who hold one of these qualifications:

  • An active RPSGT credential.
  • An active CPSGT credential.
  • An MD board certified in sleep medicine.
  • A PhD with ABSM certification.

Volunteering is unpaid. Volunteer mentors must hold a current eligible credential together with current professional registration or an applicable state license in current standing. The administrator verifies current standing before accepting a mentor.

Background screening is required before mentor approval, student contact or access to student information. The administrator uses an individualized review of convictions relevant to student safety and trust. There is no blanket automatic exclusion for a criminal history. Applicants have an opportunity to clarify or correct records before a decision.

Applying does not grant student access or create a tutoring assignment. The administrator reviews qualifications and screening before approval.

The administrator reviews volunteer applications manually and contacts applicants by email about acceptance and scheduling.

Apply to volunteer

The volunteer application asks about your qualifications, mentoring interests and availability. The administrator provides private instructions for credential verification and screening. Keep background reports and criminal-history details out of your initial email and public posts.

The volunteer signup form is being prepared. You can email the administrator to express interest while setup is completed.

Independent educational support

Sleep Pathways Guild is not a credentialing authority, ASTEP or STAR program, school, or medical provider. Mentorship does not guarantee an examination result, employment, admission, or credential eligibility. Use it alongside your education, current official references and appropriate clinical supervision.

The Guild is not affiliated with or endorsed by BRPT, AASM, AAST, ASET or ABSM. Mentor qualifications are reviewed by the administrator; an application alone does not establish approval.
