Reporting a possible correction

Include the page or app name, the exact wording or location, what appears incorrect, and the source or reasoning supporting the correction. Screenshots are helpful when a button, display, or mobile layout is involved.

Broken links and technical problems

Include the page address, button label, device type, browser, and what happened after the click. This information helps separate a code problem from a deployment or cache delay.

Response boundary

Sleep Pathways Guild provides educational support and cannot provide patient-specific medical advice, emergency assistance, credentialing decisions, or official examination rulings. Contact the relevant healthcare provider, employer, credentialing organization, or emergency service for those needs.
