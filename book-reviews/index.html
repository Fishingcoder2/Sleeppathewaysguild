How we review

Education first. Soft sell second.

Each Guild review asks the same practical questions: What does this resource help a sleep technologist understand? Who is it best for? Is the edition current? Is the topic broad or narrow? What official or version-sensitive sources still need to be checked? And is buying the book actually necessary for the learner's goal?

Product descriptions and edition facts are checked against publisher information where possible. Guild relevance is our educational assessment. Prices, sellers, formats, and availability can change, so purchase links are intentionally secondary to the review.
